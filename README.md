A one-line entry for the make.fun bounty "The 3AM Fridge Text", where an AI judge compares the texts a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, two at a time, and keeps the funnier one.

The line:

> It's 3am. You've opened me 4 times tonight looking for answers I don't stock. I contain cheese, not closure.

Instead of the usual expired-milk gag, the fridge plays the deadpan night-shift witness: it has counted the repeat visits, knows its owner is not really looking for food, and delivers the one honest inventory it can — cheese, not closure. 108 characters, one line, readable on a lock screen.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, no trailing newline). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
